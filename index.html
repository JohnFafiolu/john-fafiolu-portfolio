01 / ABOUT

Good analysis starts before the code.

I believe useful analysis is not just about producing charts, running models or writing queries. It begins with asking the right question and understanding how the data was generated.

My background in Statistics shapes how I approach problems: I examine data quality, test assumptions, choose methods that fit the question and communicate findings in a way people can act on.

My work spans business and customer analysis, statistical quality control, time-series modelling and research data analysis. I also enjoy teaching statistics and SQL, helping others build confidence in analytical thinking.
